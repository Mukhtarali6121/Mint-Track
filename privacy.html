Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how Mint-Track protects your financial data.

Last Updated: October 14, 2024

Your Data Stays With You

Mint-Track stores all your financial data locally on your device. We never collect, store, or transmit your personal financial information to our servers or any third parties.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Mint-Track ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our expense tracking application. Unlike many web applications, Mint-Track is designed with privacy as a core principle - your financial data never leaves your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Mint-Track is built with a privacy-first approach. We do NOT collect, store, or have access to:

  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, descriptions, categories, or dates
  • Personal Information: Names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses
  • Account Information: Bank accounts, credit card numbers, or financial institution data
  • Usage Data: How you use the application or your spending patterns
  • Device Information: Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Cookies: We do not use tracking cookies or similar technologies

3. How Your Data is Stored

3.1 Local Storage Only

All your financial data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your device. This means:

  • Data never leaves your device or browser
  • No data is transmitted to our servers
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • You have complete control over your data

3.2 Data Persistence

Your data persists in your browser's local storage until you:

  • Manually clear your browser data
  • Use the application's clear data function
  • Uninstall or reset your browser
  • Use browser settings to clear site data

4. Data Security

4.1 Local Security

Since your data is stored locally, its security depends on:

  • Your device's security measures
  • Your browser's security features
  • Physical access controls to your device
  • Your browsing habits and security practices

4.2 Recommendations

To keep your financial data secure, we recommend:

  • Using Mint-Track only on trusted, personal devices
  • Keeping your browser and device updated
  • Using strong device passwords or biometric locks
  • Avoiding use on public or shared computers
  • Regularly backing up your data using the export feature

5. Data Export and Portability

You have complete control over your data and can:

  • Export all your data at any time using the built-in export feature
  • Save your data in JSON format for backup or analysis
  • Transfer your data to other applications or services
  • Delete all your data instantly using the clear data function

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 External Resources

Mint-Track may load external resources such as:

  • Font Awesome: For icons (loaded from CDN)
  • Web Fonts: For typography (if used)

These resources do not have access to your financial data and are loaded according to their respective privacy policies.

6.2 No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or similar services
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Advertising networks
  • User behavior tracking tools

7. Children's Privacy

Mint-Track is not specifically designed for children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used Mint-Track, you can simply clear the browser data to remove any stored information.

8. International Users

Since all data processing occurs locally on your device, there are no international data transfers. Your data remains in your jurisdiction and under your control at all times.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Significant changes will be highlighted on our main page
  • Continued use of Mint-Track after changes constitutes acceptance

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, traditional data protection rights (such as access, rectification, or deletion requests) are not applicable. However, you have complete control over your data through the application itself:

  • Access: View all your data within the application
  • Export: Download your data at any time
  • Modify: Edit or update any information
  • Delete: Remove individual items or clear all data

11. Technical Implementation

11.1 Browser Storage

Mint-Track uses the browser's localStorage API to store your data. This is a standard web technology that:

  • Stores data only on your device
  • Is accessible only to the Mint-Track application
  • Persists until manually cleared
  • Is not transmitted over the internet

11.2 No Server Communication

The Mint-Track application operates entirely in your browser without communicating with our servers for data storage or processing. The only server communication occurs when initially loading the application files.

12. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the information provided on our main website. Since we don't collect personal data, we cannot respond to individual data requests, but we're happy to clarify our privacy practices.

13. Compliance

Our privacy-first approach means that Mint-Track complies with major privacy regulations including:

  • GDPR: No personal data collection or processing
  • CCPA: No personal information sale or sharing
  • Other Privacy Laws: Minimal data handling reduces compliance requirements

Privacy by Design

Mint-Track was built from the ground up with privacy as a fundamental principle. Your financial privacy is not just protected by policy - it's guaranteed by our technical architecture.
