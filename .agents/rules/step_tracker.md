# Step Progress Tracking Rule

Whenever implementing multi-step workflows, or whenever the user writes "step", the response MUST include a clear status section covering:

1. **Current Step**: Title, exact objectives, and all scenarios being addressed.
2. **What Has Been Done**: Bulleted list of completed steps, modifications, and verifications.
3. **What Is Remaining**: Bulleted list of pending steps in the roadmap to reach the ultimate goal.

This ensures full transparency, alignment, and execution discipline at every phase.
